MOSAIC ESCAPE

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Mosaic Escape ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game published by Thimral. This policy explains what information the game uses. Contact: Thimral.contact@thimral.com.

What stays on your device

The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress (levels cleared, stars, the mods you hold, your streak, the daily chest, the level sections you have opened) and your settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for a free mod or extra moves, through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), app interactions and diagnostic information. In the European Economic Area, the UK and some other regions we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (shown where required). You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Level sections can be opened with a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or account details. The game receives only confirmation that a purchase completed, so the section can be opened, and asks Google Play for your past purchases so they can be restored on a new phone.

Notifications

The game can remind you when your daily chest is ready and when your streak is about to lapse. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no information leaves it. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in Android's notification settings.

Sharing and rating

If you choose to share the game or a result, or to rate it, your device hands the request to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Google Play). What you send there is governed by that app's policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Watch no ads: simply don't tap a "watch a video" button.
  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy choices (where available), or your Android ad settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game. There is no account to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
